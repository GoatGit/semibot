# Contributor License Agreement (Draft)

This file is a first draft.

## Purpose

Semibot requires a Contributor License Agreement so the project owner can:

- maintain clear copyright control;
- relicense future versions if needed;
- offer commercial licensing;
- keep long-term flexibility for financing, open-core, and enterprise use.

## Agreement Summary

By contributing code, documentation, tests, examples, or other material to the
Semibot project, you agree that:

1. You have the right to submit the contribution.
2. The contribution is your original work, or you have sufficient rights to
   submit it.
3. You grant the project owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
   transferable, sublicensable, irrevocable license to use, copy, modify,
   distribute, relicense, and commercialize your contribution as part of the
   project and derivative works.
4. You understand that your contribution may be incorporated into versions of
   the project distributed under:
   - the current source-available license;
   - a future open-source license;
   - a future commercial license;
   - a dual-license model.
5. You retain copyright in your own contribution, but grant the rights above
   so the project owner can operate the project without license fragmentation.

## Corporate Contributions

If you contribute on behalf of an employer or other legal entity, you confirm
that the entity has authorized the contribution and the grant of rights above.

## No Obligation

The project owner is not required to accept, use, distribute, or credit any
contribution.

## Process

The project may require contributors to affirm this CLA through:

- a signed document;
- a click-through workflow;
- a pull request bot check;
- another written acceptance mechanism approved by the project owner.

Until that process is completed, contributions may be rejected.

This draft should be reviewed by qualified counsel before external rollout.
